**Mô tả:**
Swipelate dịch văn bản bạn bôi đen ở bất kỳ ứng dụng nào và hiện bản dịch ngay cạnh chữ, không phải sao chép hay mở tab mới.

## English (en-US)

**Short description:** Select text in any app, read the translation right there.

**Description:**
Swipelate translates the text you select in any app and shows the translation right next to it, with no copying and no new tabs.

- Select text and click the small icon, or press Alt+Q. Double-click for a word, triple-click for a sentence.
- Translate a region of the screen with OCR (Alt+W): images, videos, games, scanned PDFs.
- Many engines: Google (no key needed), DeepL, Claude, any OpenAI-compatible API (ChatGPT, Gemini, Ollama, LM Studio).
- Offline translation on your device with Opus-MT models.
- Live translation of English captions (Windows 11 22H2 or later), word meanings explained in the context of the sentence.
- Vocabulary book, translation history, clipboard board, per-app language profiles.
- Light and dark themes; runs quietly in the system tray.

Open source under the MIT License. No ads, no account.

**Features:**
- Translate selected text in any app
- Screen OCR translation
- Offline translation
- Google, DeepL, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Ollama
- Live caption translation
- Vocabulary book and history
